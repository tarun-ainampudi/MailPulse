Port 25 SMTP & Cloud Host Restrictions

Architectural Design of this Application

To deliver instant, accurate results without needing insecure backend proxies or risking spam blacklists, this app executes live DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) lookups against both Cloudflare Public DNS (1.1.1.1) and Google Public DNS (8.8.8.8).

Why Free Cloud Hosts Block Direct Port 25 Connections

Most popular cloud platforms and VPS providers—including Render, Railway, PythonAnywhere, AWS EC2, DigitalOcean, and Heroku—strictly block outbound TCP Port 25 on free or standard tiers.

  • Spam Prevention: Botnets and scrapers frequently spin up free cloud accounts to blast unsolicited spam messages. Blocking Port 25 protects provider IP reputation.
  • IP Blacklisting: Hosting providers avoid their shared IP pools being placed on Spamhaus, Barracuda, or SpamCop blacklists.
  • SMTP Greylisting: Direct socket handshakes from cloud IPs to target mail servers are often silently dropped or delayed by SpamAssassin or proofpoint filters.
